Our 12th annual Science Fair will take place on **Saturday, May 16** in the school gymnasium from 10am to 2pm.

## How to participate

- **Students:** project sign-ups open April 28 in the science classroom
- **Families:** drop in any time during the event to see the projects
- **Volunteers:** we're looking for a few parents to help with setup — email the office if interested

Last year we had over 80 student projects on display. We can't wait to see what curious minds bring this year!
